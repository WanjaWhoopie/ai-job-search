# Interview Preparation Guide

## STAR Format

Structure answers as: **Situation** (context), **Task** (your responsibility), **Action** (what you did), **Result** (outcome).

Keep answers to 1-2 minutes. Be specific. End with what you learned or would do differently.

## Ready-Made STAR Examples

### 1. Savannah Informatics Platform Reliability (Production ML / Stakeholder Impact)
**S:** A nationwide health platform serving 25M+ users showed recurring performance regressions that were hard for clinical teams to detect early.
**T:** As Analyst, I needed to surface trends automatically and translate findings into actions stakeholders could act on.
**A:** Built automated trend-analysis pipelines in Python and Looker Studio; presented model findings to clinical and cross-functional teams; triaged infrastructure issues across distributed facilities.
**R:** Contributed to a 10% improvement in platform reliability; increased system adoption by 20% through data literacy training.
**Use for:** "Tell me about a production impact," "Cross-functional collaboration," "Health tech experience"

### 2. GRA — NLP for Low-Resource African Languages (Research / Mission / RAG)
**S:** Rural communities lack accessible digital health information in local languages; standard NLP tools underperform on low-resource African language corpora.
**T:** Develop inclusive NLP solutions and a knowledge architecture that integrates authoritative health data with community language resources.
**A:** Building multilingual Knowledge Graph + RAG integrating WHO/MOH data with community corpora; iterative community-driven development focused on NCDs and indigenous nutrition.
**R:** Ongoing research delivery; positions candidate for NLP/LLM roles with social impact in Africa.
**Use for:** "Why this company/role," "NLP/LLM experience," "Working with ambiguity," "Mission-driven work"

### 3. ASD Clinical Classification Ensemble (Model Development / Evaluation)
**S:** Behavioral assessment data required reliable classification for clinical decision support.
**T:** Train, benchmark, and select the best-performing model with rigorous validation.
**A:** Engineered features from raw clinical data; trained Logistic Regression, SVM, and Random Forest; applied cross-validation; built optimized ensemble.
**R:** 93% accuracy on held-out evaluation with validated generalization approach.
**Use for:** "Walk me through an ML project," "Model selection," "Handling imbalanced/clinical data"

### 4. Omdena Medical Prescription NLP (Collaboration / NER)
**S:** Unstructured prescription text blocked downstream clinical analytics for a distributed volunteer ML team.
**T:** Build an NER pipeline and align the team on annotation and evaluation strategy.
**A:** Implemented SpaCy/Hugging Face NER pipeline; collaborated on model selection, annotation guidelines, and evaluation metrics.
**R:** Structured prescription data accessible for analytics; demonstrated remote/async ML collaboration.
**Use for:** "Teamwork," "NLP pipeline," "Open source / volunteer ML"

### 5. CMU Big Data GTA (Teaching / Spark / Scale)
**S:** Graduate students needed hands-on experience connecting distributed data infrastructure to ML workflows.
**T:** Design labs and guide students through training and evaluation at scale.
**A:** Created lab exercises on Spark MLlib with HDFS-scale datasets; taught end-to-end ingestion-to-model workflows.
**R:** Students completed distributed ML labs; demonstrates ability to explain complex systems clearly.
**Use for:** "Leadership," "Communication," "Big data," "Mentoring"
